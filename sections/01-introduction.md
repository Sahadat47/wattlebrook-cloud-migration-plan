# 1 Introduction

Wattlebrook Bank is a customer-owned Australian bank with about 1,200 staff and 400,000 customers. Many of the systems that its staff and customers rely on every day – payroll, document management, customer relationship management (CRM), the data warehouse, loan origination and internet banking – still run on ageing servers in the bank's own data centre. That hardware reaches the end of vendor support in March 2028, maintenance costs rise every year, and capacity cannot stretch to meet end-of-month peaks. At the same time, the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA) expects banks to maintain strong information security and to manage operational risk and business continuity actively [@apra234; @apra230]. The decision to do nothing is thus not a prudent one for the bank, since it is a slowly moving risk for the bank itself.

This report presents the project plan for the Legacy Systems Cloud Migration Project, which will move six of these systems to Amazon Web Services (AWS) in its Australian regions over twelve months, from 1 February 2027 to 28 January 2028, within a budget of AUD 4.5 million. The key planning challenge is to get the business-critical banking systems up and running in the cloud without losing data, and without disrupting customers or failing to meet regulatory requirements. The plan addresses this by implementing the system in three phases: the least critical internal systems are implemented first, customer facing systems are implemented last, and the lessons identified from each phase are learned before the subsequent phase is implemented.

The report is organised as follows. In Section 2, the literature review is provided on cloud migration strategies, cloud adoption in banking and the regulatory context. The project charter – including objectives, scope, success criteria and stakeholders – is described in Section 3. The work breakdown structure (WBS) is provided in sections 4-6, as well as the schedule and Gantt chart and a resource allocation and responsibility matrix. The risk, issue and quality management is covered in Sections 7 to 9. The report covers change request management, configuration management and GitHub, along with communication management plan and team coordination via Jira in sections 10-13. The report ends in Section 14.
